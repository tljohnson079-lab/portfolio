Portfolio quality standard

Every project is scored against these criteria before it earns a featured slot.

01 / PURPOSE

Problem & objective

State the environment, security/IT problem, assumptions, scope, and measurable goal. Avoid vague “I learned X” descriptions.

02 / BUILD

Technical depth

Document architecture, commands, configurations, code, tools, protocols, and meaningful implementation decisions.

03 / EVIDENCE

Proof of work

Attach screenshots, diagrams, logs, reports, repositories, detections, test results, or sanitized configuration evidence.

04 / SECURITY

Risk & ethics

Explain authorization, lab boundaries, sensitive-data handling, threat model, mitigations, and responsible testing practices.

05 / OUTCOME

Results & lessons

Show what changed, what worked, what failed, measurable outcomes, limitations, and what you would improve next.

06 / SIGNAL

Career relevance

Map the project to job-relevant skills, technologies, certifications, and role keywords without keyword stuffing.
